1: ## Used registers
2:
3: Z80 presents multiple general-purpose registers, not all of them are used in the kernel, here is the scope of each of them:
4:
5: | Register           | Scope                          |
6: | ------------------ | ------------------------------ |
7: | AF, BC, DE, HL     | System & application           |
8: | AF', BC', DE', HL' | Interrupt handlers             |
9: | IX, IY             | Application (unused in the OS) |
10:
11: This means that the OS won't alter IX and IY registers, so they can be used freely in the application.
12:
13: The alternate registers (names followed by `'`) may only be used in the interrupt handlers[^1]. An application should not use these registers. If for some reason, you still have to use them, please consider disabling the interrupts during the time they are used:
