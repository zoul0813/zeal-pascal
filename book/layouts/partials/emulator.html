Files, backups & fresh starts

Use S in Pascal to save source to H:. Saved H: files live in this browser's private filesystem, including after a page reload. Editor text still in RAM is not saved. Clearing site data removes browser saves.

Fresh starts and imports create new folders. They never overwrite your earlier work. Reload the page to restart the computer after saving in the IDE. Community TF tools are available on T:; keep your work on H:.
