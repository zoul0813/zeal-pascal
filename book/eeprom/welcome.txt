# Programming the Zeal with Pascal

Welcome to your book workbench.

Type zpascal to open the Pascal IDE (T:/apps/zpascal.bin).
Pascal libraries and units: T:/lib/
Book-owned starters: H:/book/lab/ (may be replaced by starter updates)
Save YOUR programs as new files in H:/work/, never over the starters.

Use W for a new H:/work/ file, or A (Save As) before editing a starter.
Use S to save. Unsaved editor text is lost on restart.
Updating disk images replaces T:/ or B:/, but keeps your H:/ files.
Download H: backups regularly.

Try: zpascal, tree T:/, or cat B:/autoexec.zs
