# Programming the Zeal with Pascal

Welcome to your book workbench.

Type zpascal to open the Pascal IDE.
Lesson examples: H:/book/lab/
Your own programs: H:/work/
Community apps and tools: T:/apps/ and T:/bin/

Save source with S in Pascal. H: files and completed TF/EEPROM writes persist
in this browser. Unsaved editor text does not. Keep backups of your work.

Try: zpascal, tree T:/, or cat B:/autoexec.zs
