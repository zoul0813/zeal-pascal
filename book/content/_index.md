---
title: Programming the Zeal with Pascal
---
You need a little programming experience, curiosity, and a desktop keyboard. You do not need to know Pascal, assembly, or the Zeal hardware. We will explain what you need as you use it.

The emulator boots to ZShell with community tools installed. You launch Pascal, write real source, and compile it on the emulated machine. There are no scores or automatic graders: run your program, experiment, and compare what happens with the lesson.
